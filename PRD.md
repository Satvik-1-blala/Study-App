# Product Requirements Document — Study App

## Overview
Study App is a free, student-first Android focus and productivity app that helps students study without digital distractions.

## Goals
- Reduce digital distractions during study sessions.
- Help students plan, track, and reflect on study time.
- Be free and accessible.

## Non-goals (initial)
- No paid tier in v1.
- No cloud sync in v1 (local-first).

## Target users
Students (high school / university) seeking focus tools.

## Core features (planned)
- Focus timer / Pomodoro
- App blocking / distraction control (within Android limitations)
- Task and subject tracking
- Study statistics
- Offline-first data storage

## Constraints
- Android platform limitations on app blocking (see PERMISSIONS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md).
- No root required.
